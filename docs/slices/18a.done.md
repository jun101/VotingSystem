# Slice 18a (server) — done

## Built

- Migration `2026_10_19_100001_add_suspension_reason_to_institutions`: `suspension_reason` (string 500, null) after
  `suspended_at`. Not fillable on `Institution`.
- `php artisan platform:create-admin {email}` (`CreatePlatformAdmin`): checks the email, then asks `Password` and
  `Confirm password` (hidden); refuses (exit 1, nothing created) on a bad email, an email already used (removed users
  included), different passwords, or a password under 12 or over 128 characters. Role `platform_admin`, no institution,
  email verified, hashed password, name `Platform admin`, language `fr`; prints the uuid; the password is never
  printed or logged.
- Middleware `platform.admin` (`EnsurePlatformAdmin`, 403 `forbidden`), placed in the priority list before
  `SubstituteBindings` (role, then binding, then rate limit). Limiter `platform-write`: 60 an hour per admin, shared by
  the two POST routes.
- `GET /api/v1/platform/institutions` (filters `q`, `status`, `page`, `per_page`), `POST .../{institution}/suspend`,
  `POST .../{institution}/reactivate` (`PlatformInstitutionController`, `ListInstitutionsRequest`,
  `PlatformInstitutionResource`, `PlatformInstitutionCounts`, `ChangeInstitutionSuspension`). The counts are four
  aggregate queries per page (users, voters, elections by status, latest `last_login_at`); no row of a tenant is read.
- Suspend and reactivate: row lock, state judged under the lock, change and `AuditLog` entry
  (`institution.suspended` / `institution.reactivated`, `institution_id` null, subject the institution, details
  `{reason}`, actor the admin) in one transaction; the emails are queued after commit. `AuditLog` now knows the
  `institution` subject type and writes a null institution for it.
- Emails to every owner (not managers) in the institution's language: `InstitutionSuspendedNotification` (with the
  reason, as a detail line) and `InstitutionReactivatedNotification` (no reason); French and English in
  `lang/*/mail.php`, built on `mail.action` like the other emails (button: the sign-in page).
- `error.reason` with `institution_suspended` at `POST /auth/login` and the two-factor challenge only
  (`ApiException::$reason`, rendered by `ApiErrorRenderer`).
- `VotePhase::PAUSED`: suspended institution and election `scheduled` or `open`; closed, published and archived stay
  `closed`. `GET /vote/{election}`, session (`error.phase`), ballots and submit (409 `election_not_open`, phase
  `paused`) follow. `elections:advance` skips the elections of suspended institutions (list and again under the lock).
- `make generate` run; `make lint` clean (the one existing ESLint warning in `BallotResultCard.tsx` remains).

## Deviations and notes

- Order of checks: the endpoint files say session, role, rate limit, institution (404), body, state. The tests
  (`Team::shape` compares the headers of three 404s) need the institution bound before the rate limit, so the order is
  session (401), role (403), institution (404), rate limit (429), body (422), state (409). The suspend and reactivate
  files could say so.
- `Institution` carries no tenant scope, so the implicit route binding by uuid needs no scope removal; a comment in the
  controller says why. `withoutInstitutionScope()` is used (with comments) for the counts and for the owners lookup.
- `docs/design/database.md` section 2 (institutions) does not list `suspension_reason` yet.
- Reason cleaning: line breaks and tabs become a space, other control and format characters (including bidi
  overrides) are removed, then trimmed; 3 to 500 characters.
- The suspended-sign-in log lines carry no reason.

who owns the acceptance tests (not by the coding step): `PauseTest` opened the voter session with status 200 where the
endpoint answers 201, and one test voted as Wideline, who is eligible for the group ballot that the default body leaves
out (it now uses Paul). The Slice18 suite then passed (56 tests).
